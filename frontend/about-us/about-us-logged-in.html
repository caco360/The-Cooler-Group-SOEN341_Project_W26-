About Us

Learn more about our mission and team

Meal Major is a meal planning and recipe management application designed to help users maintain a healthy lifestyle. Our mission is to make meal planning easy, enjoyable, and accessible for everyone. We believe that good nutrition is the foundation of a happy and healthy life, and we're here to support you on your journey to better eating habits.


Our team is made up of passionate individuals who are dedicated to creating a user-friendly platform that empowers people to take control of their nutrition. We understand that everyone's dietary needs and preferences are unique, which is why, by importing some calorie and nutritional data from nutrutional databases, we offer a wide range of features to cater to different lifestyles and goals.

Whether you're looking to lose weight, gain muscle, manage your weekly diet plan, or simply create some balanced recipes, Meal Major has you covered. Our web application allows you to easily create meal plans, create new recipes and track your nutritional intake.


Thank you for choosing Meal Major as your partner in health. We're excited to be part of your journey towards a healthier and happier you!
